Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MINILDelux ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information remains secure.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

Quick Summary: We collect only the data necessary to provide and improve our services. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

2

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Category Description Purpose
Account Information Name, email address, profile details provided during registration Account creation and authentication
Usage Data App interactions, feature usage, session duration, crash logs App improvement and analytics
Device Information Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers, IP address Compatibility and security
Location Data General location (city/region) based on IP address Localized content and services
User Content Information you voluntarily submit within the App Core service functionality

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial account details, precise geolocation, or health data unless explicitly required for a specific feature and with your express consent.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core functionality
  • To improve, personalize, and optimize your user experience
  • To analyze usage trends and gather statistical insights
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, support, and important notices
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

Legal Basis: We process your personal information based on your consent, contractual necessity, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations, as applicable under relevant privacy laws.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating the App (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, customer support), bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where your information may be transferred as part of the transaction.
  • With Your Consent: In any other circumstance with your explicit permission.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive data at rest
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls and authentication protocols for our team
  • Continuous monitoring for suspicious activities

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
